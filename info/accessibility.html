We want everyone who needs AP Portal to be able to use it. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we've done

  • Every page works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Form errors are announced to screen readers and linked to the field they belong to.
  • Status is shown with text as well as colour, for example "Pending approval" and "16d overdue".
  • Pages adapt to phone screens, browser zoom and your device's light or dark setting.
