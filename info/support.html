Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in page, choose Forgot password? and enter your work email. The reset link expires after 30 minutes.

I can't see an invoice I expect

Check the status filter on the dashboard and use Clear filter. Paid and rejected invoices are shown for the last 30 days only.

What do the age buckets mean?

They group unpaid invoices by how many days have passed since the due date: not yet due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and more than 90 days.
